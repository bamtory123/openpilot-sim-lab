# Limitations

This is SIL only: synthetic camera rendering, virtual CAN/Panda behavior, vehicle dynamics and timing do not validate an ECU, EPS, real CAN bus, HIL or a road vehicle. OpenPilot is the SUT; simulator-specialist experiments are separate local artifacts and do not improve the pretrained OpenPilot model. CARLA, TensorRT and Chestnut comparison are future work, not v0.1 acceptance conditions.
