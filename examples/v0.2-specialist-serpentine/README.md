# v0.2 simulator-specialist serpentine sample

Three fresh `openpilot_serpentine_v1` runs completed as `valid/pass` at 1,200 frames with no lane departure, collision, or camera drop. Lateral RMSE was 0.444557, 0.445503, and 0.444691 m (mean 0.444917 m).

This sample is limited to the local camera-only artifact, deterministic 60 m serpentine profile, seed, direction, rendering, and 2.0 m/s target. It does not prove arbitrary route, vehicle, or real-road capability.
